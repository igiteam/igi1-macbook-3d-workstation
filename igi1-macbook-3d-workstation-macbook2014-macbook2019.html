Key Improvements

  • CPU: 4 cores → 8 cores (2x more)
  • RAM: 16GB → 32GB (2x more, faster)
  • Storage: 500GB SATA (~1,500 MB/s) → 1TB NVMe (~2,800 MB/s)
  • Graphics: GT 750M → Radeon Pro 5500M (4GB/8GB) (3-4x faster)
  • Display: Bigger, brighter, better colors
  • Battery: 3-4 hours → 8-10 hours

Verdict

You are getting a machine that is:

  • 2x faster in CPU performance
  • 2x more RAM (32GB vs 16GB)
  • 2x faster storage (NVMe vs SATA SSD)
  • 3-4x faster graphics (Radeon Pro 5500M vs GT 750M)
  • 2x better battery life (8-10 hours vs 3-4 hours)
  • Better display (bigger, brighter, P3 color, True Tone)

This is the pinnacle of Intel MacBook Pros and will serve you well for the next 3-5 years of development.
